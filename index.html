How the Deal Score works

The Deal Score is a fast triage signal from 0 to 100 — it answers one question: how far below market is the opening bid? A higher score means more built-in equity relative to what the property is likely worth.

The formula

value midpoint = (est. low + est. high) ÷ 2

bid ratio = opening bid ÷ value midpoint

Deal Score = (1 − bid ratio) × 130

130 maps the (1−ratio) ∈ [0,1] range to a 0–130 display, then clamps to 1–99 (see data.js).

…clamped to the 1–99 range

Worked example

Opening bid$52,000
Est. value band$118k – $139k
Midpoint$128,500
Bid ratio52,000 ÷ 128,500 = 40%
Deal Score(1 − 0.40) × 130 ≈ 77

What the score means

The score is triage, not truth.

It ignores liens, back taxes, repair costs, occupancy, and redemption rights — which is exactly what the AI "here's the catch" analysis on each listing covers. The value band comes from assessed value, last sale × price index, and ZIP-level home values. Always confirm with your own comps before bidding.
