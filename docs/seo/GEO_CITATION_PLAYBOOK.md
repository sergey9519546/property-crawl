## 2. The 4 Pillars of AI Citation Dominance for `property-crawl`

### Pillar 1: Statutory Citation Anchoring (E-E-A-T Moat)
LLMs weight content that cites primary legal statutes higher than generic blog summaries. Every state and county docket page (`/states/[state]` and `/states/[state]/[county]`) embeds deterministic statutory references from `server/ai/legal-rules.js`:
- **New Jersey**: `N.J. Ct. R. 4:65-5` (10-day objection/redemption window)
- **Florida**: `Fla. Stat. § 45.0315` (Right of redemption extinguishes upon Certificate of Sale filing)
- **Federal IRS Liens**: `26 U.S.C. § 7425(d)` (120-day federal redemption right)
- **Automatic Bankruptcy Stay**: `11 U.S.C. § 362`

### Pillar 2: Quotable Data Triplets (Entity-Attribute-Value)
AI retrieval pipelines extract concise, structured statistics. County pages (`src/lib/county-seo.js`) expose deterministic rollups in both visible HTML and `ItemList` / `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD:
- **Active Auction Count** (`stats.totalCount`)
- **Median Opening Bid** (`stats.medianOpeningBid`)
- **Median Risk-Adjusted Deal Score** (`stats.medianDealScore`)
- **Verified Publisher Breakdown** (`sheriff`, `civilview`, `hud`, `treasury`, `irs`)

### Pillar 3: "Answer-First" Semantic Formatting
Every public guide (`/resources`, `/about`, `/states`) is structured so the first 40 words under each `<h2>` directly answer the high-intent conversational prompts mapped in [5_STAGE_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md](file:///c:/Users/serge/.minimax/sessions/mvs_33469e4a8e48472faf25c6f55a29c489/workspace/property-crawl/docs/marketing/5_STAGE_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md):
- *Prompt*: `"Does a foreclosure wipe out a second mortgage or tax lien?"`
- *Direct Answer Block*: Foreclosing on a junior mortgage or HOA lien does **not** extinguish a recorded senior first mortgage (`/resources` Catch #1). Municipal property taxes and utility charges hold super-priority and survive the auction (`Catch #2`).

### Pillar 4: Zero-Fabrication Provenance
AI search engines penalize lead-gen farms that serve zombie listings. `PerfectProperty` exposes live source timestamps, `dataMode` transparency banners, and direct publisher notice links on every record—establishing high domain trust (`CITE` score).
