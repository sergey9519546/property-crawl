# 5-Stage Customer Awareness Journey: Distress Property Discovery & Triage
> Generated by **Marketing Strategist** under Chief of Staff | Powered by `customer-journey` skill

---

## Stage 1: Unaware

### User Mindset
- The user is priced out of retail MLS/Zillow real estate. They see sky-high interest rates and unaffordable inventory.
- They assume the only way to buy property is through traditional realtors, or that foreclosures are "only for corrupt banks or shady insiders."
- They don't know government property dispositions (HUD, US Marshals, IRS, USDA) are open to public retail bidding.

### Conversational Search / AI Prompts (What They Type)
- *"Why is buying a house so expensive in 2026?"*
- *"Can ordinary people buy government seized houses?"*
- *"Are foreclosure houses actually cheaper than normal houses?"*
- *"How do real estate wholesalers find houses 50 percent off?"*

### Content Strategy Recommendations
- **Top-of-Funnel Guides**: "How Everyday Investors Buy Government-Sold & Distressed Homes at 40% Off."
- **Infographic / Social Teardowns**: Side-by-side comparison of MLS Retail Price vs. Sheriff Sale Opening Bid on actual sold parcels.
- **Narrative Lead**: Demystify government disposition programs (HUD REO, IRS, Treasury auctions) as statutory public sales, not secretive insider clubs.

---

## Stage 2: Problem-Aware

### User Mindset
- The user knows foreclosure auctions exist and wants to bid, but realizes the process is fragmented, opaque, and terrifying.
- They've spent hours digging through outdated county clerk websites, PDF scanned notices, and broken auction vendor portals.
- They are worried about hidden second mortgages, municipal utility liens, and losing earnest money deposits.

### Conversational Search / AI Prompts (What They Type)
- *"What happens if you buy a foreclosure with an IRS tax lien?"*
- *"Why do foreclosure websites show properties that are already sold?"*
- *"How do I find upcoming sheriff sales in my county without paying a scam service?"*
- *"What is a statutory redemption period in sheriff auctions and can the owner take the house back?"*
- *"How to know if a foreclosure auction is a first mortgage or second mortgage?"*

### Content Strategy Recommendations
- **Pain-Point Deep Dives**: "The 5 Deadly Traps of County Sheriff Sales (And How to Check Lien Priority Before You Bid)."
- **State-by-State Statutory Guides**: County-specific explainers on redemption periods, upset bid timelines, and cash-to-close statutory fees.
- **Calculator Tools**: Interactive Statutory Cash-to-Close & Redemption Carry Simulator.

---

## Stage 3: Solution-Aware

### User Mindset
- The user knows they need specialized software or an automated discovery tool.
- They have tried Foreclosure.com, PropertyRadar, Auction.com, or RealtyTrac, but were disappointed by stale zombie data, fake auction countdowns, or lack of fine-print legal analysis.
- They are actively seeking software that verifies official county court records and extracts title encumbrances automatically.

### Conversational Search / AI Prompts (What They Type)
- *"Best software for finding county sheriff sales and tax deed auctions"*
- *"PropertyRadar alternatives for distressed property research"*
- *"How to scrape county public legal notices for foreclosure deals"*
- *"Foreclosure tool that analyzes title liens and opening bids automatically"*
- *"Is there an AI that reads foreclosure notices and flags bad titles?"*

### Content Strategy Recommendations
- **Comparison Pages & Tear-Downs**: "PerfectProperty vs. Foreclosure.com vs. Auction.com: Live County Record Verification."
- **Feature Teardowns**: Showcase the AI Notice Parser extracting exact character-span citations and lien priorities directly from statutory legal notices.
- **Free County Directories**: High-ranking programmatic state and county directory pages (`/states/[state]/[county]`) indexing active sales with zero paywalls.

## Stage 4: Product-Aware

### User Mindset
- The user has landed on `PerfectProperty`. They see the interactive terminal, live listings, and the Deal Score.
- They want proof: Is this live data? Does it cover my specific county? Is the Deal Score accurate? What is "The Catch"?
- They are weighing whether to sign up for saved hunts and full dossier exports.

### Conversational Search / AI Prompts (What They Type)
- *"Is PerfectProperty accurate for Florida sheriff sales?"*
- *"What is Deal Score in PerfectProperty and how is it calculated?"*
- *"PerfectProperty reviews foreclosure auction software"*
- *"Can I export county parcel cadastral boundaries from PerfectProperty?"*

### Content Strategy Recommendations
- **Transparent Data-Mode Badge**: Prominently display source timestamps, live county refresh cadences, and zero-fabrication guarantees.
- **Interactive Dossier Previews**: Allow 1-click exploration of real verified property dossiers showing cadastral parcel boundary overlays and statutory carry deadlines.
- **Honesty Guarantee**: Contrast `PerfectProperty`'s evidence-first "Catch" callouts against competitor black-box valuations.

## Stage 5: Most Aware

### User Mindset
- The user loves the platform and uses it weekly or daily to prep for courthouse auctions.
- They want automated alerts, instant webhook/email delivery when high-scoring deals appear in their target zip codes, and multi-user workspace access.

### Conversational Search / AI Prompts (What They Type)
- *"PerfectProperty pricing and saved hunts setup"*
- *"How to set up automated email alerts for new sheriff sales in Orange County FL"*
- *"PerfectProperty API access for auction data"*

### Content Strategy Recommendations
- **Streamlined Workflow Onboarding**: 2-click saved hunt configuration (`/hunts`) with custom criteria thresholds (e.g. Deal Score $\ge 85$, opening bid $< \$200\text{k}$).
- **Instant Triage Export**: Clean 1-page PDF deal sheets designed for courthouse bidding floors.
