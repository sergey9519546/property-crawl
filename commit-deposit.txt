fix(parse): accept colon-prefixed Deposit: $X form

The deposit regex in routes/parse.js required "Deposit $X" or
"Deposit of $X" but real notices also use "Deposit: $X" and
"Required deposit: $X". Without a colon slot in the regex, the
deposit_terms field came back null for those notices and the LLM
prompt lost the deposit context.

Repro: extractObservedNotice on a notice containing "Deposit: $5,000
cashier check" returned deposit_terms=null even though opening_bid
and other money fields extracted cleanly.

Fix: add a colon to the separator slot. The colon is optional, so
the existing "Deposit $X" and "Deposit of $X" matches still work and
the new "Deposit: $X" form now extracts. Added a regression test on
the existing SAMPLE_NOTICE which already used the colon-prefixed
form but was never asserted against.
