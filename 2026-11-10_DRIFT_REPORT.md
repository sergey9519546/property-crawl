## docs/STRATEGY.md

* **Statutory Alert Pipeline (Hack #1)**: Architecture describes automated 24/7 background IMAP polling daemon connecting directly to statewide press association feeds (`floridapublicnotices.com`, `publicnoticeillinois.com`). Codebase implements parser and EML intake engine (`server/ai/notice-parser.js`, `server/scrapers/email-intake.js`, `src/components/workspace/review-split-pane.tsx`), but 24/7 background IMAP daemon remains policy-deferred to maintain $0 zero-credential footprint.
* **Multi-County Vendor Parsers (Hack #2)**: Architecture specifies platform-level parsers for CivilView, Realauction, and AcclaimWeb. Codebase implements all three platform adapters (`server/scrapers/civilview.js`, `server/scrapers/adapters/realauction.js`, `server/scrapers/adapters/acclaimweb.js`). However, `server/scrapers/sheriff.js` contained a block-scoping defect in `fetchCountyRealauction` where `accumulatedListings` was scoped inside `for (const targetDate of datesToTry)`, triggering a caught `ReferenceError` on date continuation. (Repaired in 2026-11-10 resolution).
* **Self-Healing Ingestion (Hack #3)**: Architecture describes automated prompt mutation based on extraction failures. Codebase implements deterministic fallback parsing and split-pane human-in-the-loop review queue (`src/components/workspace/review-split-pane.tsx`, `server/routes/document-review.js`), while autonomous prompt rewriting without human review remains policy-deferred.
* **Free Enrichment Stack (Hack #4)**: Architecture specifies Census geocoder, County ArcGIS REST endpoints, FHFA HPI, Socrata code enforcement, HUD SAFMR, and bulk Zillow/Redfin CSV ingestion. Codebase implements Census (`census-geocoder.js`), ArcGIS (`arcgis-parcels.js`), FHFA HPI (`fhfa-state-hpi.js`, `hpi.js`), Socrata (`code-enforcement.js`), and HUD SAFMR (`hud-safmr.js`). Monthly bulk Zillow/Redfin CSV ingestion remains deferred to prevent exceeding free-tier storage limits.
* **Commercialization & Subscriptions (Hack #5)**: Architecture describes paid Stripe subscription webhooks ($19-$49/mo) and automated instant-match email delivery. Codebase implements programmatic SEO pages (`src/app/sheriff-sales/`), statutory underwriting simulator (`src/components/terminal/bidding-simulator.tsx`), title risk engine (`server/intelligence/title-risk-engine.js`), and Resend email forms (`src/lib/resend.ts`), while live Stripe billing webhooks remain policy-deferred.
* **Vehicle Vertical**: Architecture mentions secondary vehicle auction vertical via GSA Auctions API (`gsa.github.io/auctions_api`). Codebase enforces real-estate category filtering (`server/scrapers/adapters/gsa-api.js`), deliberately excluding personal property to maintain real estate focus.
